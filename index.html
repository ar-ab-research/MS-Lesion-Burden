Mouse and keyboard
  • Click or drag in a view to move all three.
  • Scroll, or ↑ ↓, steps through slices. Page Up and Page Down step ten.
  • Shift and an arrow key moves the crosshair within the slice.
  • [ and ] go to the previous and next lesion.
  • L shows or hides lesions. O switches fill and outline.

Research use only. Not a medical device, and not for diagnosis.
